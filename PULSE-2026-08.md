## Method, honestly

- Counted from employers' own applicant-tracking systems (13 platforms), not job boards — one observation per employer per day, deduplicated by posting id.
- *Net* = postings appearing minus postings disappearing across the window. A disappearance means filled **or** withdrawn; we do not guess which.
- The panel grows as employers are onboarded; aggregate totals are never compared across days — only the like-for-like series is.
- Every figure is machine-generated from the archive; the archive's integrity is hash-chained daily and externally anchored.
