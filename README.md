# ASX Hiring Pulse

A monthly read on hiring across ASX-listed companies, measured where hiring
actually happens: each company's own careers system, observed daily. The count comes from the employers' own postings rather than job
boards or surveys, observed and differenced day by day.

**Latest edition: [2026-09](PULSE-2026-09.md)**

## What this is

Since August 2026 the underlying panel has tracked 60+ ASX-listed employers
across 13 applicant-tracking platforms, one observation per company per day.
The Pulse publishes the monthly summary: who is adding roles, who is quietly
removing them, advertised pay where platforms disclose it, and a like-for-like
trend that is never contaminated by panel growth.

- [`sample_day.csv`](sample_day.csv): one real day of the panel, 12 companies
- [`DICTIONARY.md`](DICTIONARY.md): every field, the method, and the stated limitations

## Why it can be trusted

The archive behind this is append-only with per-file hash manifests, chained
daily and anchored externally, so every published figure traces to a capture
that provably existed on its stated date. Collection gaps are disclosed in the
data rather than inferred from absence. Full methodology in the dictionary.
